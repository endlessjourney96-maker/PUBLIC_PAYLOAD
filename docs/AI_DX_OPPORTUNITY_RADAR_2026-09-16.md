## Trigger to reconsider priority

Re-score the B2B AI-DX candidate only when at least one of these is true:

1. the current MVP produces genuine funnel evidence and reveals a repeated organizational/workflow pain;
2. an inbound lead or concrete buyer conversation creates a credible short path to paid work;
3. a major platform/rule change materially damages the current acquisition or monetization path;
4. a new opportunity can be tested with existing assets in <= 1 working day and without delaying current CV measurement.

Until then, do not expand features, landing pages, SNS channels, paid acquisition, affiliate activation or UOM scope merely to chase the market signal.
